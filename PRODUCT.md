# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Gomatch is used by students and course teammates in a practical training setting. Users are players who want a lightweight LAN/online Gomoku client for logging in, matching, playing, chatting, spectating, checking records, and adjusting settings without operational friction.

## Product Purpose

Gomatch provides a JavaFX desktop client and Socket-based server for real-time Gomoku matches, rooms, rankings, records, and social features. Success means the client feels reliable, easy to scan, and cohesive enough for repeated use during coursework demos and team testing.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, restrained. The interface should feel like a polished student-built product: clean and modern, but never visually noisy or overly decorative.

## Anti-references

Avoid saturated arcade colors, heavy dark cards, rough gradients, default system-blue focus rings, cluttered settings controls, and inconsistent one-off component styles.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the white minimalist frosted-glass visual language across pages.
- Favor familiar product UI affordances over novelty unless motion directly communicates interaction state.
- Keep controls compact, readable, and aligned with the existing 8px radius and bottom-line vocabulary.
- Use neutral slate and charcoal tones for emphasis; reserve semantic color for status and warnings.
- Ensure settings and navigation remain easy to scan during repeated use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Interactive controls should expose clear hover, pressed, focused, and disabled states. Text should maintain readable contrast against translucent surfaces, and motion should be short, state-driven, and non-blocking.
